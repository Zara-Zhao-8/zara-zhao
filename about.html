Hi! I'm glad you made it to my corner of the internet! Read on to learn about my background, interests, & some things that I've been thinking about.

Student @ UChicago

I'm a rising third year studying mathematics and economics, but I've always had an interest in design with a focus on helping others.

Broadly, the courses I study in university have taught me how to think and how to approach challenges with holistic reasoning, while also enjoying the process! The problems I find the most stimulating are ones that begin ambiguous and involve many different factors to think about. This way, I’m able to studying the edges around each puzzle piece before fitting together the entire picture.

Tinkering with My Eyes and My Hands

I like working with my hands and exploring different mediums, but my eyes do most of the work by helping me spot needs in different environments! Otherwise, I wouldn’t see my charger cables are tangled and need organizing, I wouldn’t be able to compare similar products with each other to pick the best fit, and I wouldn’t be able to help my friends create products that benefit them. These hobbies are creative ways of expressing what I love to do - designing and creating things with people in mind!
