About

hi, i'm zara.

3D artist, sometimes painter, full-time collector of half-finished sketchbooks.

I grew up taking apart things I wasn't supposed to — remote controls, an old radio, a beloved dollhouse — mostly to see how the pieces fit. Twenty-some years later I'm still doing that, just in ZBrush instead of the garage.

I work mainly in 3D: sculpting organic forms, building small imagined worlds, and occasionally animating them just enough to feel alive. Before that I trained in traditional painting, which still shows up in how I light and texture everything — I think in glazes and gradients even when I'm pushing polygons.

My process is slow and a little obsessive. I'll spend a week on a single material study if it means getting the light right. I care more about a piece feeling true than feeling polished — the small imperfections are usually the part I like best.

These days I split my time between freelance client work, personal pieces, and writing about all the ways a project can go sideways before it goes right. This site is where I keep all of it in one place.
