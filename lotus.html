Red Lotus

This project was to practice creating and 3D printing non-linear shapes. I had a bit of trouble thinking about how to orient the surface of the flower. In the end, I printed the flower facing upwards, so that no supports were needed during the printing process.

Dimensions for the lotus petal.

Revolving it into a rounder shape.

Sweeping out the excess material to get a nicely curved petal.

Creating a circular pattern around the origin for a beautiful lotus.

Adding the center!
