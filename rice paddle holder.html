Rice Cooker Paddle Holder

The rice cooker I bought for college didn't come with a side handle, meaning that after meals, the only option for my paddle to go was on top of the cooker. This would result in extraneous cooked rice bits on the top, which was hard to clean. Instead of buying a whole new rice cooker, I decided to make a paddle holder to attach to my dish rack, such that my paddle could have a place to stay after being used.

Setting up the dimensions of my holder.

Extruding the sketch into the main body.

Sketching the dimensions of the small hook that would attach to the dish rack and extruding this to have a sturdy thickness. Note that the rice cooker is upside down so that 3D printing becomes easy!
