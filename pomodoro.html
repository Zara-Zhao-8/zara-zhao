Pomodoro Timer

I wanted a way to make studying more stimulating for me, and I thought an entertaining round little orange might just be the solution to focusing for longer. This project combines my interest in product design with my need for a better study tool. This project is still in progress, and I am currently working on the electrical engineering part of customizing the arduino.

Setting up the sketch concept with determined parameters.

Using the revolve command to create the main body.

Adding stem and leg details. I wanted this orange to have a friendly appearance!

Making room for the internal structure of the electronic components. Also created precise slots for small magnets to hold the halves in place.
