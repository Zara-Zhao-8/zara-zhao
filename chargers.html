Charger Organizer

I'm not above losing track of what cables connect to which one of my devices, so I built this organizer to help my desk stay organized.

This was my first prototype. When using it, I noticed that it did not securely hold my chargers - the spaces were too wide such that the chargers could rotate and slide out, and the ridges seemed too flimsy to last long. I decided to re-iterate by creating thicker ridges while narrowing the space for the charging cable.

Here are the changes below! Wider support ridges and a thicker platform for the cables to hold.

Here is the final product! It is the same design as the first prototype, but it is much sturdier and practical for the desk space.
